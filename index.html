About Dune 18

Relaxed coastal luxury on Long Beach Island.

Located at 1916 Long Beach Blvd in Ship Bottom, Dune 18 blends modern seaside comfort with warm, attentive hospitality. Whether you join us in our chic indoor dining room or enjoy the ocean breeze on our al fresco outdoor patio, every evening is crafted to feel effortless and memorable.

From fresh Barnegat Bay shellfish and Thai Coconut Seared Scallops to dry-aged steaks and seasonal Prix Fixe menus, our kitchen celebrates the vibrant flavors of the Jersey Shore.

BYOB & Local Bellview Winery Selections Bring your favorite bottle of wine or craft beer—we gladly provide decanters, ice buckets, and stemware—or select a bottle from New Jersey's Bellview Winery right at your table.
